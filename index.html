Research Papers
Multi-Agent RL Economics Under Review, Lake Como AI/ML Systems 2025

Recovering Strategic Phase Structure in Recurrent Multi-Agent Socio-Economic Systems

Submitted to AI/ML Systems Conference (Lake Como) 2025  ·  ASDRP, mentored by Dr. Phil Mui

Naive recurrent deep Q-networks fail to recover strategic phase structure in economic multi-agent settings through three systematic failure modes: reward-scale domination, credit assignment collapse across factorized instruments, and recurrent overwrite of sparse strategic signals. This paper identifies those failure modes and introduces FactorDRQN , an architecture with factorized action heads, a tau-conditional stateless retaliation pathway, and decomposed Bellman targets, achieving 92.2% cell-level regime agreement on Deterrence, Transition, and Escalation phase recovery.

Multi-Agent RL Trade Policy Under Review, IEEE CIFEr 2025

Regime Structure in Adaptive Tariff Conflicts: A Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Analysis

Submitted to IEEE CIFEr 2026  ·  ASDRP, mentored by Dr. Phil Mui

A multi-agent reinforcement learning framework calibrated to the 2018-19 US-China trade episode. The central finding: across an 8×8 structural parameter grid, the Deterrence-Escalation boundary tracks a single compact predictor, follower retaliation capacity divided by leader tariff ceiling, with striking consistency. A second finding reveals genuine coordination sensitivity near structural boundaries: identical parameters produce divergent stable outcomes across learning runs, suggesting multiple attractors whose selection depends on early exploration paths.

Environmental ML Explainable AI Synopsys Science Fair 2026 2025-26

ClariFIRE: Explainable AI-Driven Wildfire Hazard and Community Risk Mapping

Independent Research  ·  Presented at Synopsys Science Fair 2026

A statewide, interpretable wildfire hazard system built on a self-constructed California geospatial dataset (2004-2025) integrating satellite, climate, drought, and terrain data. Under realistic unbalanced testing at true California fire prevalence (~0.49%), ROC-AUC reached 0.94. Under strict temporal holdout (training 2004-2020, testing 2022-2025), ROC-AUC ranged 0.77-0.81, with the top 20% highest-risk areas capturing 51-66% of actual wildfire events. SHAP explainability applied globally and at county level; hazard predictions integrated with CDC Social Vulnerability Index to generate community risk maps.

Climate Aviation Under Review, JHSS 2024-25

Improving Aviation Contrail Prediction and Avoidance Using Satellite Observations

Independent Research  ·  Synopsys Science Fair 2025  ·  Under peer review, Journal of High School Science

Combined in-flight humidity measurements with satellite-based contrail observations to correct systematic errors in atmospheric prediction models, improving contrail forecast accuracy from 75% to 85%. Analysis of targeted altitude adjustments on a subset of flights demonstrated a 40-45% reduction in contrail climate warming contribution through refined prediction and rerouting analysis.
