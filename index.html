Multi-Agent RL Trade Policy Accepted  ·  IEEE CIFEr 2026 2025-26

Regime Structure in Adaptive Tariff Conflicts: A Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Analysis

IEEE CIFEr 2026  ·  Presenting in Tokyo, September 2026  ·  Mentored by Dr. Phil Mui

A multi-agent RL framework calibrated to the 2018-19 US-China trade episode. The central finding: across an 8x8 structural parameter grid, the Deterrence-Escalation boundary tracks a single compact predictor (follower retaliation capacity divided by leader tariff ceiling) with striking consistency. A second finding reveals genuine coordination sensitivity near structural boundaries, where identical parameters produce divergent stable outcomes across learning runs.

Multi-Agent RL Economics Under Review  ·  ACM/IEEE AI-ML Systems 2026 2026

Recovering Strategic Phase Structure in Recurrent Multi-Agent Socio-Economic Systems

ACM/IEEE 6th Intl. Conference on AI-ML Systems, 2026  ·  ASDRP, mentored by Dr. Phil Mui

Identifies three systematic failure modes in recurrent deep Q-networks applied to strategic economic settings: reward-scale domination, credit assignment collapse across factorized instruments, and recurrent overwrite of sparse strategic signals. Introduces FactorDRQN, an architecture with factorized action heads, a tau-conditional stateless retaliation pathway, and decomposed Bellman targets, achieving 92.2% cell-level regime agreement on Deterrence, Transition, and Escalation phase recovery.

Environmental ML Explainable AI Synopsys Science Fair 2026 2025-26

ClariFIRE: Explainable AI-Driven Wildfire Hazard and Community Risk Mapping

Independent Research  ·  Presented at Synopsys Science Fair 2026  ·  Paper in preparation

A statewide, interpretable wildfire hazard system built on a self-constructed California geospatial dataset (2004-2025) integrating satellite, climate, drought, and terrain data. Under realistic testing at true California fire prevalence (~0.49%), ROC-AUC reached 0.94. Under strict temporal holdout (training 2004-2020, testing 2022-2025), ROC-AUC ranged 0.77-0.81, with the top 20% highest-risk areas capturing 51-66% of actual wildfire events. SHAP explainability applied globally and at county level, integrated with CDC Social Vulnerability Index.

Multi-Agent RL Game Theory Accepted — SCCUR 2025 2025

Analyzing Socio-Political Reasoning Using Multi-Agent AI Trained on Societal Dilemmas

Accepted for presentation at Southern California Conference for Undergraduate Research (SCCUR) 2025  ·  ASDRP, mentored by Dr. Phil Mui

A modular multi-agent RL platform implementing Q-learning across four environments: Repeated Prisoner's Dilemma, Stackelberg, Diner's Dilemma, and Senate voting. Large-scale experiments compare learned multi-agent behavior against static game-theory and Nash baselines, analyzing how memory and coalition rules shift equilibrium outcomes away from Nash predictions.

Climate Aviation Synopsys 2025 2024-25

Improving Aviation Contrail Prediction and Avoidance Using Satellite Observations

Independent Research  ·  Synopsys Science Fair 2025

Combined in-flight humidity measurements with satellite-based contrail observations to correct systematic errors in atmospheric prediction models, improving contrail forecast accuracy from 75% to 85%. Analysis of targeted altitude adjustments on a subset of flights demonstrated a 40-45% reduction in contrail climate warming contribution.
